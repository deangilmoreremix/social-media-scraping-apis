# Ultimate Affiliate Manager Research Tool Plan
## Transforming Submagic Intelligence Platform

---

## Your Role as Affiliate Manager

You need to:
1. **DISCOVER** hundreds of potential affiliates monthly
2. **QUALIFY** them efficiently (fit, reach, conversion potential)
3. **OUTREACH** at scale with personalization
4. **TRACK** performance and relationships
5. **OPTIMIZE** based on what's working
6. **REPORT** to leadership on program ROI

---

## Phase 1: Universal Discovery Engine

### Multi-Source Creator Prospecting

**A. Hashtag/Keyword Discovery**
- Input: Keywords like "video editing," "content creation," "TikTok tips"
- Scrape ALL creators using those hashtags across 15 platforms
- Auto-rank by follower count, engagement, content quality
- Export to CRM-ready CSV

**B. Competitor Affiliate Reverse-Engineering**
- Input: Competitor brand (e.g., "CapCut," "VEED")
- Find ALL creators who promoted them
- Analyze which creators drove most engagement
- Prioritize those NOT yet promoting Submagic

**C. Trend-Jacking Discovery**
- Input: Trending sounds, viral formats, hot topics
- Find early adopters who jump on trends quickly
- These creators are experimental = more likely to try Submagic

**D. Lookalike Creator Finder**
- Input: 5-10 successful Submagic affiliates
- Find creators with similar:
  - Audience demographics
  - Content style
  - Engagement patterns
  - Growth trajectory
- AI-powered similarity scoring

## Phase 2: Deep Qualification System

### 360° Creator Vetting Dashboard

**A. Content Intelligence Layer**
```
Video Transcription → Keyword Analysis → Niche Classification
                    ↓
    "Mentions video editing?" ✓
    "Tutorial style content?" ✓  
    "Audience asks 'how you edit?'" ✓
                    ↓
         HIGH QUALITY LEAD
```

**B. Engagement Quality Score**
- Fake follower detection (sudden spikes, bot patterns)
- Comment sentiment analysis (real questions vs spam)
- Engagement velocity (consistent vs viral spikes)
- Audience response to previous affiliate posts

**C. Competitive Positioning**
- What editing tools do they CURRENTLY use?
- Have they promoted competitor tools before?
- What's their price sensitivity (based on promoted products)?
- Are they exclusive to any brand?

**D. Conversion Readiness Indicators**
- Do they already use captions/subtitles? (shows need)
- Do they mention "productivity" or "workflow"? (shows pain point)
- Do they have "link in bio" culture? (shows affiliate readiness)
- Do they reply to comments? (shows engagement = trust)

## Phase 3: Intelligence & Market Research

### Competitive Intelligence Module

**A. Competitor Affiliate Mapping**
- Track ALL creators promoting CapCut, VEED, Descript, etc.
- Identify which creators are "tool reviewers" vs "occasional promoters"
- Find creators who promoted competitors but engagement was LOW (they need better tool)
- Monitor competitor affiliate program changes (commission rates, new features)

**B. Market Trend Analysis**
- Track mentions of "AI captions," "auto subtitles," "video accessibility"
- Identify rising demand in specific niches (education, real estate, fitness)
- Spot emerging platforms where Submagic could be first-mover

**C. Pricing Intelligence**
- What commission rates are competitors offering?
- What materials are they providing affiliates?
- What's working for them (high-engagement posts)?

## Phase 4: Outreach Automation System

### Personalized Outreach at Scale

**A. Auto-Generated Outreach Sequences**

Based on creator analysis, auto-generate:
```
Email 1 (Day 0): Personalized hook based on their content
- "Loved your TikTok on [specific video] about [topic]"
- Submagic value proposition tailored to THEIR pain point
- Free access offer

Email 2 (Day 3): Social proof from similar creators
- "Creator X in your niche made $X with Submagic"
- Specific example relevant to their content style

Email 3 (Day 7): Materials provided
- Custom affiliate materials based on their top-performing content
- "Here's a script that matches your style"

Email 4 (Day 14): Final follow-up + feedback request
```

**B. Multi-Channel Outreach**
- Email (primary)
- LinkedIn DM (for business-minded creators)
- Twitter DM (for casual/startup creators)
- Instagram DM (for visual creators)

**C. Contact Enrichment**
- Find email from Linktree, bio, LinkedIn cross-reference
- Find phone for WhatsApp (high-value creators)
- Find manager/agent contact (for large creators)

## Phase 5: Campaign Management & Tracking

### Affiliate Lifecycle Management

**A. Pipeline Stages**
```
DISCOVERED → ANALYZED → CONTACTED → RESPONDED → 
ACTIVATED → PERFORMING → CHAMPION → DORMANT
```

Each stage has:
- Automated triggers
- Required actions
- Conversion probability
- Suggested next steps

**B. Performance Tracking**
- Track each creator's affiliate link clicks
- Track conversions attributed to each creator
- Track content performance (views, engagement on affiliate posts)
- Calculate ROI per creator

**C. Automated Alerts**
- Creator posted affiliate content → Alert + performance prediction
- Creator engagement dropping → Nurture sequence trigger
- High-performing creator → Upsell to higher commission tier
- Dormant creator → Re-activation campaign

## Phase 6: Research Workflows

### Pre-Built Research Templates

**Workflow A: Weekly Prospecting Sprint**
1. Monday: Run hashtag scrapers for 10 relevant keywords
2. Tuesday: Auto-analyze top 50 creators with AI
3. Wednesday: Review qualification scores, approve top 20
4. Thursday: Auto-send personalized outreach
5. Friday: Review responses, move to pipeline

**Workflow B: Competitor Intelligence Report**
1. Input: 3 competitor brands
2. Scrape all their affiliates
3. Identify overlap with your target list
4. Generate "opportunity gap" report (creators they have, you don't)
5. Prioritize by creator quality

**Workflow C: Campaign Performance Analysis**
1. Pull all affiliate posts from last 30 days
2. Analyze which materials performed best
3. Identify which creator types convert highest
4. Generate recommendations for next campaign
5. Auto-create new materials based on winners

**Workflow D: Event-Based Prospecting**
1. Input: Industry event (e.g., "VidCon," "Creator Economy Expo")
2. Scrape all attendees posting about event
3. Filter for video creators
4. Fast-track outreach: "Saw you're at VidCon..."
